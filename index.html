Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Will PDF to Word keep my layout?

It extracts the text of each page into an editable document. Complex layouts, tables and images are not preserved, and scanned PDFs need OCR, which is not supported.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but very large files depend on your device's memory.

Does it work offline?

You need an internet connection the first time to load the conversion libraries.
